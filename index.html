Privacy Policy — AEGIS Market Risk Intelligence

  • Contact: mailtoadishreegupta@gmail.com
  • Effective date: 2 October 2026

1. What the app does

AEGIS Market Risk Intelligence follows published reports ("market stories") about the companies in India's NIFTY 50 index:

  • who reported a story first;
  • which later reports are copies;
  • what the company has said about it, as reported in the news;
  • how much evidence there is.

It is an information tool. It is not investment advice. It does not recommend buying or selling, cannot place trades, and does not handle money or payments.

2. Information stored on your phone

  • Your settings:
  • whether you finished the welcome screens;
  • Simple View, larger text and appearance (light or dark).
  • Test server details, if you enter them: the address of an AEGIS test server, and the access code you were given. The access code is kept in Android's encrypted storage (Android Keystore).
  • Copies of stories you viewed, so the app also works without a connection.

This information stays on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it.

3. Information sent from your phone

  • By default the app uses the stories that come with it and sends nothing.
  • If you connect it to an AEGIS test server (only possible with an address and access code from the developer), the app requests stories from that server over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Each request carries your access code, so the server knows you are an invited tester.
  • What the server records: for operation and security, for each request:
  • which page was requested;
  • the result;
  • the time;
  • a scrambled (one-way hashed) form of your network address.

It does not record your access code or your unscrambled network address. These records are kept only in size-limited log files that are overwritten automatically.

  • Never collected:
  • your name, email address, phone number or contacts;
  • your location, photos or files;
  • any device identifier.
  • No tracking components: the app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking components.
  • No selling or sharing: nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

4. Links to other websites

Each story lists its sources. Opening one takes you to that publisher's website in your browser, where that website's own privacy policy applies.

"See the price chart on TradingView" opens TradingView's website (tradingview.com) in your browser or the TradingView app. AEGIS sends nothing to TradingView: the only information in the link is the company's stock symbol. What TradingView collects is covered by TradingView's own privacy policy.

5. Children

The app is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

6. Deleting your data

For AEGIS Market Risk Intelligence by Adishree Gupta.

  • Data on your phone: uninstall the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → AEGIS Market Risk → Storage → Clear storage. This deletes everything the app keeps: your settings, saved stories, and any test-server address and access code. Nothing is kept.
  • Records on the AEGIS test server (only if you connected the app to one): email mailtoadishreegupta@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my AEGIS data". All of the server's request records (the page requested, the result, the time and the scrambled network address) are deleted within 30 days of your email. Nothing is kept, and AEGIS holds no other data about you.
  • Without a request, those server records are kept only in size-limited log files that are overwritten automatically.
  • To stop sending requests: remove the server address under More → Where AEGIS gets its information. The app then uses its built-in stories.

7. Security

  • Connections to the test server use HTTPS. The server requires an access code and limits how often it can be called.
  • The access code stored on your phone is encrypted by Android.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling information differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
